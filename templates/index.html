Chain of custody

Every verdict is traceable, end to end.

This isn't a claim-in, verdict-out chatbot. Each investigation runs through nine independent agents, and every one of them persists its own status and output.

01 — 02

Extract & analyze

Pulls the checkable factual claim out of your input and classifies it.

03 — 04

Research in parallel

Searches the live web and your knowledge base simultaneously for evidence.

05 — 06

Weigh the evidence

Sorts supporting vs. contradicting evidence and scores each source's credibility.

07

Argue the other side

A dedicated adversarial pass searches specifically for evidence the claim is false.

08

Verify

Computes a verdict and confidence score — never a guess when evidence is thin.

09

Report

Assembles a citable, exportable report with every source attached.

Built against hallucination

It would rather say “unverifiable” than guess.

∅

No evidence, no verdict

If a search returns nothing, the verdict is unverifiable — never a confident-sounding guess.

↯

Adversarial by design

A dedicated agent runs a second round of searches specifically hunting for evidence that the claim is false.

⚖

Credibility is a weight, not a veto

A low-credibility source lowers confidence in evidence drawn from it — it never single-handedly flips a verdict.
